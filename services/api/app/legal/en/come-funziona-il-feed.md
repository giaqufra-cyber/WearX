# How the feed works

The Digital Services Act (art. 27) asks platforms to explain the criteria they use to select
and rank content. Here are ours, with nothing hidden.

## What goes into your feed

- Only fits from the **styles you've joined** (or the style you pick at the top), posted in the
  **last 14 days**.
- What **doesn't** go in: your own fits, the ones you've already rated, those from people you've
  blocked or who have blocked you, those hidden by moderation and those not suitable for your
  age group.
- The people you follow **don't** change the feed: you find them on their profiles.

## In what order

Every fit has a score that combines two things:

1. **Quality:** the community's average score, kept "cautious" while there are few scores (it
   starts at 60 and the real average weighs more and more as scores come in). Anomalous scores
   don't count and those from new or unverified accounts count half.
2. **Freshness:** a fit that has just been posted is worth more; the advantage halves roughly
   every day, but never drops below a third.

The score is recalculated every 5 minutes.

## Room for newcomers

One slot in five goes to a freshly posted fit (less than 48 hours old and fewer than 10
scores), picked at random: that way even people posting for the first time get seen and rated.

## What we don't use

We don't use any data about you beyond the styles you choose and the fits you've rated: no age
(apart from the protections for 16-17 year olds), no location, no time spent looking at a fit,
no data from other apps, no advertising.

## You can change the feed

- Pick your styles in **Explore**: adding or removing one changes the feed straight away.
- At the top of the feed you can look at **just one style** at a time.
- Every style page shows that style's fits with the same criteria, the same for everyone.

## Does rating change the order?

Your score goes into the fit's average, which updates roughly every hour and only from 5 scores
upwards: that's why a single score doesn't move the order straight away.
