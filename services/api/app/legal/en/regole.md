# Community Guidelines

On WearX you rate the style, not the person. These rules apply to fits, photos, captions,
nicknames, profile photos, bios and links. We apply them to everyone in the same way.

## Rate, don't insult

- Rate the fit: the combinations, the idea, the care. Not the body, age or looks of whoever is
  wearing it.
- No comments on WearX is a choice: the score is enough.
- Organising to push someone's scores down or up, using several accounts or automated programs
  is forbidden. We spot anomalous scores and they don't count.

## What to post

- **Your fits:** photos that show the outfit, taken by you or with the permission of whoever
  took them.
- **In the right style:** if most of the community answers "no" to "Is this really this
  style?", the fit leaves the style's page (it stays on your profile).
- **People who agree:** if other recognisable people are in the photo, they must be on board.

## What isn't allowed

These are the reasons you see when you report something:

- **Nudity or sexual content.** Underwear and swimwear are fine as part of an outfit; nudity
  and sexual poses are not.
- **Harassment or threats.** Against anyone, even outside WearX. No hate towards groups of
  people.
- **Minors at risk.** Any content that sexualises or endangers a minor: immediate closure of
  the account and a report to the authorities.
- **Stolen photos.** Other people's photos passed off as your own, photos taken from other
  profiles or sites.
- **Wrong style.** Content deliberately put in the wrong style or that isn't a fit.
- **Dangerous links.** Scams, sites that steal data, counterfeit products, malware.
- **Spam.** Repeated advertising, chain posts, fake accounts, swapping scores or follows.
- **Anything else illegal.** Content that is illegal under Italian or European law.

## What happens when the rules are broken

- The content may be hidden, removed or moved to another style.
- **Strikes:** a warning the first time, 7 days without posting the second time, closure of the
  account the third time (the last 12 months count).
- For the most serious violations, closure is immediate.
- Every decision comes with its reasons. If you think it's wrong you can appeal from the app
  within 6 months: a different person from the one who decided will review it.

## Reporting

Tap the report button on the fit or the profile and choose the reason. We never tell anyone
who reported. Reports about minors at risk are reviewed within an hour, those about nudity and
harassment within 24 hours, the rest within 72 hours.
