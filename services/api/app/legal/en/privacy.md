## In short

- We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. The app has no cookies and no third-party
  tracking tools.
- Others see your nickname, profile photo, bio and fits (each with its style). Only you see the
  styles you've joined. Your email and phone number stay private.
- Others can find you by searching for your nickname and see you among "people you may know".
  You can take yourself out of search: then only someone typing your exact nickname finds you.
- Scores are anonymous: whoever gets a score doesn't know who gave it. In our database scores
  are stored under a pseudonym, not with your account.
- We only use your date of birth to work out your age group and we don't keep it.
- The data is stored in the European Union (Ireland and Belgium).

## What data we process

**Account.** Email (or phone number) and password. The password is handled by the sign-in
service and nobody at WearX can read it. When you choose a password, the app checks it isn't
among those exposed in known data breaches: the Have I Been Pwned service only receives the
first 5 characters of a fingerprint (hash) of the password, never the password.

**Profile.** Nickname, profile photo (if you add one), bio, account type (private or
Business), chosen styles (only you see them), settings (hidden prices, hidden number of
scores, being findable in search, notifications, language), the version of the Terms you
accepted and the date you accepted them.

**Search and suggestions.** To suggest people you may know we use who you follow, who follows
you, who the people you follow follow, and styles in common. Next to a suggestion we may show
up to two people you follow who follow them ("Followed by @…") or how many styles you have in
common, never which ones. We remember who you removed from suggestions, so we don't suggest
them again. The profiles you open from search ("Recent") stay only on your phone.

**Saved and archive.** Only you see the fits by others that you save: whoever posted them sees
how many times a fit of theirs was saved, never by whom, and gets no notification. Nobody but
you sees a fit of yours that you've archived, but it is kept (with its scores and items) until
you put it back on your profile or delete it.

**Age.** We need your age group (16-17 or 18 and over) and the date you'll turn 18. The date of
birth you enter is used to work these out and then we delete it. If your age is verified by an
external provider (selfie estimate, ID document, SPID or CIE, when available), we only receive
the result from them: no images, no documents, no date of birth.

**Fits.** Photos, caption, style, items worn (brand, name, price, shop link) and, if you add
it, the place. We strip all hidden data from photos (GPS location, phone model, date taken). For each photo we also keep a
numeric fingerprint, used to recognise photos already removed for serious violations if
someone tries to post them again.

**Place of the fit (optional).** You choose it by searching for its name: a venue, a shop, a
street or a city (16-17 year olds can only add the city). It's shown with the fit to whoever can
see your profile: if your profile is private, only your followers (it never appears on anonymous
fits in the feed). You can change or remove it whenever you want. We never use your phone's
location and home addresses can't be chosen. Our server runs the search with Photon (komoot,
OpenStreetMap data): it only receives the text you type, not your IP address or your account.

**Scores.** A score from 1 to 100 and, sometimes, the answer to "Is this really this style?".
They are stored under a pseudonym calculated with a secret code, not with your account. They
are used to prevent double scores and to spot anomalous ones (for example many identical
scores); anomalous scores don't count towards averages.

**People.** Who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, accounts you've blocked.

**Reports and moderation.** The reports you make (reason and details), the moderation
decisions that concern you, with their reasons, and your appeals.

**Notifications and devices.** The notifications you receive; for push notifications, the code
your phone gives us to deliver them and the app language on that phone. For each sign-in:
phone model (e.g. "iPhone 15"), operating system, app version, first and last use, so you can
see and end sessions from Privacy and security > Connected devices.

**Device integrity.** To limit automated accounts and scores, the app asks Apple (App Attest)
or Google (Play Integrity) to confirm that it's the real app on a real phone. We keep the
result and, on iPhone, a public key of the device.

**Statistics (Insights).** When someone sees or opens a fit, opens a profile or taps a shop
link, we record the event under a pseudonym (different from the one used for scores), without
IP address and without phone identifiers. Statistics only show aggregate numbers and hide
values from 1 to 4 people.

**IP address.** We use it, without saving it in the database, to limit repeated attempts on
some features (for example the nickname check): it stays in memory for a few minutes.

**Errors and technical logs.** When something breaks we record the type of error and the
request path. Logs contain no email, phone, password, IP address or content; in a few
technical cases (for example a failed security check) they contain the account's numeric
identifier.

## Automated decisions

Some things are decided by a program:
- the **order of the feed** (explained in [How the feed works](come-funziona-il-feed));
- the **weight of scores** (lower for brand-new accounts or unverified phones, zero for
  anomalous scores);
- **checking photos** against the list of those already removed and checking texts against
  banned words;
- **hiding a fit straight away** when several serious reports arrive from different people,
  until a moderator looks at it.

None of these has legal effects on you. Decisions that restrict you (removal, suspension) are
made or confirmed by a member of our staff, we always explain why and you can appeal from the
app.

## How long we keep it

- **Account and profile:** for as long as you keep it. If you ask for deletion, the account
  disappears for others straight away and you can change your mind for 30 days; after that we
  permanently delete your photos and data.
- **What remains after deletion, with no link to you:** the scores you gave (inside the
  averages of other people's fits; their pseudonym is replaced with a random one, so they can
  no longer be linked to you), the moderation decisions and the reports made (we need them for
  the transparency reports required by law).
- **Photos removed for serious violations:** we only keep their numeric fingerprint, to stop
  them being posted again.
- **Unused or rejected uploads:** deleted within 7 days.
- **Events for statistics:** 3 months, then deleted; only the daily totals remain.
- **Archive of your data (export):** downloadable for 7 days, then deleted.
- **Age verification in progress:** expires after 1 hour. An "under 16" result from an ID
  document blocks new attempts for a year.
- **Push notification codes:** deleted when you sign out, end a device session or the phone no
  longer accepts them.
- **Limits on repeated attempts (IP address):** a few minutes.
- **Technical logs and errors:** [30] days.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use,
receive it in a readable format (portability) and object to uses based on legitimate interest.
You can do many things yourself in the app, in **Privacy and security**:
- **Download your data:** an archive with everything we keep about you, photos included;
- **Delete account;**
- **Connected devices:** see and end sessions;
- **Notifications:** choose which ones to receive;
- **Hide prices, Hide the number of scores, Let people find you in search, Account type,
  Language.**

For anything else write to privacy@wearx.app: we reply within a month. If you think we are
processing your data unfairly you can complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority
(**Garante per la protezione dei dati personali**, www.garanteprivacy.it) or to the authority
of the EU country where you live.

## If you're 16 or 17

WearX is only for people aged 16 and over. For 16 and 17 year olds: the profile starts out
private, fits are only visible to other 16-17 year olds, adults can't search for you or see
you in suggestions, you only appear in search if you choose to (and only to other 16-17 year
olds), we don't suggest adults with a private profile to you, the place of a fit can only be a
city, some styles are reserved for
adults, you don't get notifications between 10 pm and 7 am and photos are checked with stricter
criteria. When you turn 18 your age group changes by itself; fits posted before stay visible
only to 16-17 year olds.

## Security

Encrypted connections, private photo storage (photos only open with expiring links), staff
access with two-factor authentication and a log of every lookup, secrets kept in a dedicated
vault, automatic security checks on every code change.
