# Deleting your WearX account

## From the app (the quickest way)

1. Open WearX and go to your profile.
2. Open **Privacy and security** and tap **Delete account**.
3. Type your nickname to confirm.

From that moment your profile and your fits disappear for everyone and your other devices are
signed out. For **30 days** you can still change your mind by signing in again; after that we
permanently delete your photos and data.

## Without the app

Write to **privacy@wearx.app** from your account's email address (or stating the phone number
you signed up with) with the subject "Account deletion". We'll ask you to confirm the account
is yours and delete it under the same rules.

## What remains after deletion

With no link to you: the scores you gave (inside the averages of other people's fits, under a
random pseudonym), and the moderation decisions and reports made, which the law requires us to
keep for transparency reports. All the details are in the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
